Unlock the Experience

At LuxeFlow, we make accessing exclusive content simple, organised, and convenient. Follow the steps below to understand our ordering and delivery process.

01. Account Verification

Before accessing our collection, users must complete the verification form with accurate and valid information. All submitted details must be genuine.

Providing deliberately false, misleading, or fraudulent information may result in account termination and restricted access to LuxeFlow.

02. Explore the Collection

Once verified, you can explore our exclusive collection, discover available content, and select the items that interest you.

Take your time, browse through the available options, and create your selection.

03. Finalise Your Order

After selecting your desired content, proceed to finalise your order. Review your selection carefully before confirming it.

04. Storage & First-Time Purchase

For first-time customers, the selected content will be provided through a physical storage device — either an SD Card or a Pen Drive.

You can arrange your own compatible storage device or purchase one through LuxeFlow.

After completing your order, contact our Support Team for information regarding device pricing, specifications, compatibility, and purchasing instructions.

05. Storage Device Payment

If you choose to purchase your SD Card or Pen Drive through LuxeFlow, its cost must be paid in advance, in cash, before your content order is dispatched.

If you prefer, you may arrange your own suitable storage device instead.

06. Order Delivery & Payment

Once your order is delivered, the remaining order amount must be paid in cash upon delivery, according to the agreed payment arrangement.
